# Contributing / 参与贡献

Issues and pull requests are welcome for measured mechanical clearances, printable tolerances, calibrated optical results, screenshots of the actual assembly, and alternative video sources.

**Please distinguish measurements from estimates.** For print-fit reports, share the acrylic's measured width/thickness, printer, material, slicer settings, and the measured screen-to-plate gap. For optics, share ambient lighting, phone brightness, viewing angle, and reflection/ghosting results.

Use the MIT license for contributions. Do not submit third-party portrait photos, logos, video or other content without rights to publish them.

Keep the V0.2 geometry and instructions reproducible; propose significant redesigns separately rather than silently changing the meaning of published STL variants.
